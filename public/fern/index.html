Specifications

Crafted to the Finest Detail

Premium materials, reputed hardware, and intelligent infrastructure — every element built for lasting excellence.

Category 01

Structure & Walls

5 details
  • RCC framed structure
  • External Walls: 8-inch-thick solid CC blocks
  • Internal Walls: 4-inch and 8-inch-thick solid CC blocks
  • External Finishes: Double coat plastering in cement mortar
  • Internal Finishes: Single coat plastering in cement mortar, finished with gypsum punning

Category 02

Flooring & Painting

9 details
  • Drawing / Dry Kitchen / Dining / Living: Imported marble
  • Master Bedroom / Home Theatre: Laminated / Engineered wood
  • Other Bedrooms: Vitrified tiles
  • Staircase: Imported marble / granite
  • Toilets: Vitrified tiles
  • Balconies and Terraces: Vitrified tiles / Anti-skid tiles
  • Parking and Driveway: Interlocking tiles / Stamped concrete / Vitrified tiles
  • Internal: Putty finish with two coats of premium emulsion paint over one coat of primer
  • External: Two coats of weatherproof paint. Smooth / texture surfaces and cladding as per Architect's design

Category 03

Doors & Windows

7 details
  • Main Door: Aesthetically designed 9' height teak wood / engineered wood door frame with veneer shutter finished with PU polish & of reputed hardware make
  • Internal Doors: Aesthetically designed 8' height teak wood / engineered wooden door frame with veneer shutter finished with PU polish and hardware of reputed make
  • Sliding Doors: Powder-coated / anodized aluminium doors with clear toughened glass and mosquito mesh
  • Windows: Powder-coated / anodized aluminium windows with clear toughened glass and mosquito mesh
  • Provision for video door phone
  • Railings with toughened glass for balcony & staircase; MS railing for terrace as per Architect Design
  • Provision For Lift (Lift under customer Scope)

Category 04

Kitchen, Electrical & Media

5 details
  • Provision for modular kitchen, water purifier, and gas piping
  • Concealed copper wiring of reputed make with modular switches
  • Ample power points and provision for EV charging point
  • Provision for internet
  • TV Point in all bedrooms, living room, lounge & home theatre

Category 05

Plumbing & Bath

4 details
  • Premium pipes for internal and external works
  • Reputed make sanitary ware and CP fittings in bathrooms
  • Provision for solar water heater / heat pump / solar Power
  • Provision for Jacuzzi in Master Suite toilet

Security

  • 24/7 Secured Living Environment
